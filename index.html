Why you need this Safari on iPhone freezes JavaScript when the screen locks. The only way to get notified with screen off is to have a server do the checking and push an alert to your phone. UptimeRobot is a free service that does exactly this — it monitors a URL every 5 minutes from their servers and sends you an email or app notification when it changes.
